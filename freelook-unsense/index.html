👁️ Freelook

Look around. Keep walking straight.

Hold Left Alt and your camera moves on its own while your character keeps facing forward. Check behind you while sprinting, look at the sky while elytra flying, or watch your back while bridging, then let go and the camera snaps right back.

✅ Fabric · Minecraft 26.1 → 26.3 · client-side only

💬 Found a bug or have an idea? Tell us on Discord.

✨ Features

  • 🎥 Free camera: turn the camera all the way around while your body keeps its direction.
  • 🔁 Hold or toggle: hold the key (default), or press once to turn it on and again to turn it off.
  • 👀 Pick the view: start freelook in third person behind you, in front of you, or stay in first person.
  • 🖱️ Feels like normal looking: the same mouse speed as your normal camera, or faster / slower if you want.
  • ↩️ Snaps back: your old camera view comes back the moment you let go.
  • ⌨️ Rebindable in Options → Controls → Key Binds → Freelook.
  • 🪶 Tiny & client-side: works on any server, nothing to install there.

⚙️ Configuration

config/freelook.json is created on the first start:

{
  "mode": "hold",
  "perspective": "third_person_back",
  "sensitivity": 1.0,
  "maxPitch": 90.0
}
Option What it does
mode hold or toggle
perspective Camera when freelook starts in first person: third_person_back, third_person_front or first_person
sensitivity Mouse speed while freelooking (1.0 = normal)
maxPitch How far up / down the camera can turn (90 = straight up / down)

📦 Installation

  1. Install Fabric Loader and Fabric API.
  2. Put Freelook.jar in your mods folder.
  3. Start the game and hold Left Alt.
