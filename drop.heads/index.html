💀 PlayerHeadDrop

Kill a player. Take their head.

Every PvP kill drops the victim's player head with their real skin as loot. Collect your rivals, show off your trophies, and give fights on your server something to win.

✅ Spigot, Paper & Bukkit · 1.19 → 1.21.9 · zero dependencies

💬 Found a bug or have an idea? Tell us on Discord.

✨ Features

  • 💀 Real skins. The dropped head shows the victim's actual skin, not a default Steve.
  • ⚔️ PvP only. Heads drop only when a player is killed by another player. Mobs, falls and lava drop nothing.
  • 🏆 Trophies that matter. Place heads on walls, put them in item frames or trade them. Your kill count is on display.
  • 🪶 Lightweight. One tiny jar with no config, no database and no background tasks.
  • 🔌 Zero setup. No commands, no permissions to set up. Install and it works.

🎮 Perfect for

  • PvP & factions servers: bounties, raids and wars with proof of the kill
  • SMP servers: a fun reward for settling arguments the old-fashioned way
  • Events & tournaments: hand out the heads of the defeated as prizes

📦 Installation

  1. Download the jar.
  2. Put it in your server's plugins/ folder.
  3. Restart the server.
  4. That's it. The next PvP kill drops a head.

📋 Requirements

  • Spigot, Paper or Bukkit 1.19 – 1.21.9
  • No other plugins needed
