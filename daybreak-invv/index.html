🌅 Daybreak

Sleep through the night the way it should feel: the stars spin by, the sun races up over the hills, and everyone wakes to a warm "Good morning".

Not everyone has to be in bed. Set a percentage, leave AFK players out of the count, and watch a smooth time-lapse instead of vanilla's sudden jump to morning. Server-side only. No resource pack, no mods, no dependencies. Paper and Folia, 1.20 → 26.x.

✨ Features

🌙 Sleep percentage that makes sense

  • Choose how many players must sleep, from one player to everyone (default 50%).
  • AFK players don't block the night. Anyone idle for 5 minutes is left out of the count, until they move, chat or click.
  • Spectators and vanished players (SuperVanish, PremiumVanish, EssentialsX…) are left out too. Creative players optionally.
  • daybreak.ignore leaves staff or bots out completely.
  • Getting into bed always counts, even for someone who was AFK a second ago.

⏩ A real time-lapse, not a jump cut

  • Once enough players are asleep, the night plays out in about 8 seconds: the moon sets, the stars wheel overhead and the sun climbs over the horizon.
  • It spins up smoothly and eases into the sunrise instead of slamming into it.
  • Every sleeper helps: even before there are enough, each player in bed makes the night run a little faster.
  • Someone gets out of bed? The night slows back down to normal, no harm done.
  • Works with shaders: the sky, clouds and shadows sweep across the world in one fluid motion.

📊 Everyone knows what's going on

  • A boss bar shows ☾ 2/3 sleeping · 03:40, the clock and how much of the night is left. It turns gold once the night is being skipped.
  • An action bar says how many more players are needed (and replaces vanilla's confusing text).
  • A short chat message when someone goes to bed, with an anti-spam cooldown for bed jumpers.

☀️ A morning worth waking up for

  • A "Good morning" title with the day number and how many nights your server has slept through.
  • A soft chime, clear skies, and well-rested players get a few seconds of Regeneration.
  • Phantoms leave sleepers alone, and optionally everyone in the world.
  • Daytime thunderstorms can be slept away too, with their own "Clear skies" greeting.

🛡️ Clean and safe

  • No game rules are changed. Daybreak blocks vanilla's jump-to-morning with an event, so removing the plugin leaves your world exactly as it was.
  • Folia native: time and weather run on the global region, everything that touches a player on that player's own thread.
  • One light task for the whole server, nothing scanning the world.
  • Per-world: whitelist or blacklist worlds. The Nether and the End are never touched.

⌨️ Commands

Command Description Permission
/daybreak status [world] Who is sleeping, how many are needed, AFK count, clock, day, weather, nights slept through daybreak.status
/daybreak skip [world] Skip the night (or the thunderstorm) right now daybreak.admin
/daybreak reload Reload config and messages daybreak.admin

Aliases: /db, /sleepstatus. daybreak.ignore (off by default) leaves a player out of the count. daybreak.notify (op) tells admins about updates.

⚙️ Configuration

sleep:
  percentage: 50
  ignore:
    spectators: true
    creative: false
    vanished: true
    afk-seconds: 300

time-lapse:
  seconds: 8             # how long a skipped night takes
  partial-speedup: 3.0   # each sleeper speeds up the night a little (1 = off)
  ramp-seconds: 1.0

storms:
  skip-thunderstorms: true

morning:
  clear-weather: true
  title: true
  sound: 'minecraft:block.note_block.chime'
  well-rested:
    - 'regeneration:1:8'
  reset-phantoms-for-everyone: false

display:
  boss-bar: true
  boss-bar-color-waiting: blue
  boss-bar-color-skipping: yellow
  action-bar: true
  bed-message: true
  bed-message-cooldown: 15
  twelve-hour-clock: false

Every message is MiniMessage, so gradients, colors and symbols are all yours. English and Polish are included, and you can add your own lang/<name>.yml. /daybreak reload applies everything live.

🔌 PlaceholderAPI

Placeholder Value
%daybreak_sleeping% Players in bed
%daybreak_needed% Players that must be in bed
%daybreak_missing% How many more are needed
%daybreak_afk% Players left out for being AFK
%daybreak_time% The clock, e.g. 21:30
%daybreak_day% Day number
%daybreak_nights% Nights slept through in this world
%daybreak_skipping% true while the night is being skipped

🧑‍💻 For developers

NightSkipEvent (cancellable) fires right before a night or storm is skipped. MorningEvent fires at sunrise with the day number and the players who slept.

@EventHandler
public void onSkip(NightSkipEvent event) {
    if (bossFightRunning(event.getWorld())) event.setCancelled(true);
}

@EventHandler
public void onMorning(MorningEvent event) {
    event.getSleepers().forEach(id -> giveDailyReward(id));
}

📦 Compatibility
Servers Paper, Folia, Purpur and other Paper forks
Versions 1.20 → 26.x
Java 17+ (21+ for 1.20.5 and newer, as Minecraft itself requires)
Dependencies None. PlaceholderAPI optional
Clients Vanilla. Nothing to install

Plain Spigot is not supported: Daybreak uses Paper's schedulers and text APIs.
